Evidence first. Decisions stay human.

Policy signalA finding mapped to a defined market, carrier, platform or advertiser profile for review.
Human decisionAn authorised operator determines whether to pass, edit, restrict, schedule or escalate the title.
Versioned contextRules and title records can change; dates and source links show which evidence supported the analysis.
