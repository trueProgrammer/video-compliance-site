Enterprise FAQ

Questions compliance and operations teams ask.

Answers abstracted from real enterprise evaluations, covering architecture, rules, integrations, scale, outputs and governance.

Ask another question →
Is Vidcomply a cloud-native platform or an infrastructure layer?

Both. Use Vidcomply Studio as a self-serve platform or embed the AWS-deployed compliance layer through REST APIs, webhooks and customer-controlled storage.

How do dynamic rules and global compliance profiles work?

Vidcomply separates observable evidence from policy. Configure territory, platform, carrier, brand and house-rule profiles, adjust categories and severities, then re-evaluate affected assets without retraining the detection models.

How does Vidcomply integrate with an existing media supply chain?

Submit assets or storage references through REST APIs, S3 and enterprise connectors. Receive job status by webhook and return evidence as JSON, reports, marker files or editorial outputs.

Can Vidcomply scan websites, CMS materials and other enterprise sources?

Yes. Vidcomply can review static pages, defined dynamic or authenticated journeys, CMS and AEM materials, documents, images and structured content. Findings can identify language discrepancies, conflicting or outdated information and policy issues with URL, field, passage, DOM or screenshot evidence. Private sources require authorised access configured during onboarding.

Can Vidcomply process large libraries and related files together?

Yes. Run bulk independent jobs or evaluate related video, audio and subtitle assets together. Elastic AWS processing scales with the workload; turnaround depends on runtime, formats, ingress method and the agreed service level.

What does a compliance result include?

Results can include the exact timecode or file location, representative frame, category, severity, policy reason, confidence, proposed action and reviewer decision—delivered through reports, JSON and timeline-ready outputs.

How does Vidcomply monitor accuracy and handle feedback?

Performance is reviewed with precision, recall, false-positive and false-negative measures, validation datasets and configured acceptance criteria. Reviewer feedback remains attached to the decision and can tune rules and thresholds.

Does Vidcomply replace legal or editorial approval?

No. Vidcomply produces evidence-backed policy signals and operational outputs. Qualified customer teams retain final editorial, legal and release authority.
